What is greenhouse irrigation automation?

Greenhouse irrigation automation brings water to your greenhouse and puts it on timers and zones, similar to the zones in your yard. Drip lines feed each planter automatically, replacing daily manual watering.

Why Greenhouse Irrigation Matters in Kingman and Northwest Arizona

A greenhouse concentrates plants that depend on you for every drop of water — automation keeps them watered on schedule whether you are home or away.

Different Than Your Yard

Greenhouse plants grow in planters and beds under cover, so rain never reaches them. They need their own dedicated water supply, not just what your outdoor yard system provides.

Plant Loss While Away

In summer heat, a greenhouse without automated watering can lose plants in days. Timed watering keeps everything alive so you can go away without losing your greenhouse.

The Manual Watering Burden

Hand-watering every planter is a daily chore that never takes a day off. Automation removes that burden and waters more consistently than a hose ever will.

Automated Timers

Timers run your greenhouse like zones in your yard — set the schedule once and each planter gets watered on time, every time.

Our Greenhouse Irrigation Solutions

Automated watering built for your greenhouse, from the water source to each individual planter.

Automated Drip Delivery

Drip lines route water directly to each planter and bed inside your greenhouse, delivering it slowly at the roots without hand-watering.

Timer & Zone Setup

We install timers and divide your greenhouse into watering zones — similar to the zones in your yard — so each area runs on its own schedule.

Greenhouse-Specific Water Needs

Covered planters, mixed plant types, and summer heat all change how much water a greenhouse needs. We set flow and schedules to match what is actually growing inside.

Yard System Integration

We can bring water to your greenhouse from your existing yard irrigation, tying it into the system you already have.
